# Mental Models of J. Brandon Dixon

Core conceptual lenses used by J. Brandon Dixon to analyze self-pumping vascular systems and bioengineering design.

## The Lymphatic Vessel as an Intrinsic Cardiac-like Pump
Rather than treating collecting lymphatic vessels as passive conduits or open drainage pipes, view them as chains of autonomous, pressure-sensitive muscle pumps (lymphangions). When upstream fluid volume or downstream pressure increases, intact vessels elevate their contraction rate and force—analogous to an elevated heart rate. Chronic overwork under elevated afterload leads to cellular exhaustion, oxidative stress, smooth muscle remodeling, and eventually, cardiac-like pump failure.
> "Think of it like an elevated heart rate for a lymphatic vessel"
(sources: src_009, src_010, src_017, src_023)

## Asynchronous Volumetric Lymphangion Chambers
In valved peristaltic vessels, fluid transport is driven by treating inter-valve vessel segments as dynamic, variable-volume chambers bounded by elastic one-way valves. When peristaltic contraction waves travel at a wavelength mismatched with valve spacing, consecutive valves open and close out of phase. This creates dynamic inter-segment pressure differentials that pump fluid volumetrically across adverse pressure gradients rather than relying solely on progressive wall contraction waves.
> "When the valve spacing does not match the wavelength, the valves open asynchronously and the volume of the vessel segments bounded by two consecutive valves changes periodically, thereby inducing volumetric fluid pumping."
(sources: src_026)

## Lymphatic-on-a-Chip
Recreating microscale vascular architectures and extracellular matrix environments on microfluidic platforms to isolate biological and physical variables. By culturing human endothelial cells under controlled fluid shear stress and transmural pressure, researchers can dissect cellular responses, parasite interactions, and drug permeability in vitro without systemic confounding factors.
> "So we develop engineering technologies, either through imaging or through recreating the lymphatic environment on sort of a Petri dish type setting to study lymphatic biology and function."
(sources: src_012)
