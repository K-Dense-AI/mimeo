# Principles of J. Brandon Dixon

Overview of core principles guiding J. Brandon Dixon's bioengineering research, mechanobiology analysis, and clinical device strategies.

## Compensatory Overwork Drives Long-Term Lymphatic Remodeling and Failure
When surgical trauma or elevated fluid loads burden a vascular network, intact remaining vessels compensate by increasing contractile frequency and force. However, this sustained workload triggers oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, and smooth muscle wall thickening, ultimately causing delayed pump breakdown years after initial injury.
> "A computational model of a chain of lymphatic contractile segments incorporating these adaptations predicted increases in the flow-generation capacity of the remodelled vessel at the expense of normal mitochondrial function and elevated oxidative stress within the lymphatic muscle."
(sources: src_010, src_017, src_018, src_023)

## Multidisciplinary Engineering Integration in Biological Research
Solving complex self-pumping biological problems requires integrating fluid mechanics, cellular biology, computational modeling, signal processing, and dynamic imaging. Single-discipline approaches fail to account for how molecular signaling pathways dynamically interact with physical shear stress and pressure gradients.
> "Dixon's laboratory learn to weave together techniques in molecular and cell biology, biomechanics, imaging, computer programming, and image and signal processing to provide insight into the regulation of lymphatic physiology."
(sources: src_000, src_001, src_012, src_015)

## Active Functional Assessment Over Static Anatomical Imaging
Evaluating vascular health or therapeutic efficacy demands measuring dynamic transport metrics—such as contraction rate, packet velocity, and occlusion pressure—rather than relying on static structural vessel identification or tissue staining.
(sources: src_009, src_021)

## Combinatorial Interventions for Complex Secondary Lymphedema
Severe secondary lymphedema involves a multi-factorial cascade of mechanical pump failure, persistent inflammation, tissue fibrosis, and adipose deposition. Effective treatments must pair pro-lymphangiogenic or contractile-enhancing agents with anti-inflammatory strategies.
(sources: src_010)

## Physiologically Relevant Lymphedema Animal Models
Preclinical models must preserve partially intact drainage pathways and respect gravitational loads to faithfully mimic clinical pathophysiology, where remaining intact vessels handle overload and undergo compensatory remodeling.
> "now we made one minor adaptation that we think increases through the physiologic or clinical relevance of this model and that is that we have we ligate one side and we leave one side intact"
(sources: src_010, src_017)

## Asynchronous Valve Operation Enhances Peristaltic Volumetric Pumping
In valved peristaltic vessels, spacing elastic one-way valves out of sync with contraction wavelengths creates cyclic inter-valve volume changes that significantly increase net fluid transport against pressure gradients.
> "When the valve spacing does not match the wavelength, the valves open asynchronously and the volume of the vessel segments bounded by two consecutive valves changes periodically, thereby inducing volumetric fluid pumping."
(sources: src_026)

## User-Centered Simplicity in In Vitro Device Design
In vitro microfluidic platforms and organ-on-a-chip models must prioritize operational ease so non-engineering biological and clinical collaborators can independently operate and reproduce experiments.
(sources: src_009)

## LEC-Targeted Nanomedicine for Transient Lymphatic Regeneration
Delivering therapeutic agents (such as VEGFC mRNA) specifically to lymphatic endothelial cells using targeted lipid nanoparticles promotes localized, transient growth without triggering systemic side effects or uncontrolled vascularization.
> "Our results suggest a therapeutic potential of VEGFC mRNA lymphatic-specific targeted delivery in alleviating lymphatic dysfunction observed during lymphatic injury and could provide a promising approach for targeted, transient lymphangiogenic therapy."
(sources: src_022)

## Democratizing Clinical Diagnostics via Automated Surface Reconstruction
Coupling mobile 3D surface acquisition with machine learning enables objective disease classification independent of expert clinical availability, expanding diagnostic reach in low-resource environments.
> "can we utilize deep learning, machine learning approaches to make a disease classification independent of the expertise required for one clinician, solely from the surface reconstructions that we can create from these patients."
(sources: src_015)
