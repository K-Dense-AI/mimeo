# Anti-Patterns to Avoid

Explicit practices, design traps, and assumptions rejected in Linda Griffith's bioengineering philosophy.

## Relying Exclusively on Rodent Models for Immunomodulatory Drugs
Testing immunologically focused therapeutics primarily in mouse models leads down translational dead ends because human immunology, macrophage lineages, and reproductive biology differ fundamentally from rodents.

> "We've cured endometriosis in a lot of mice and a lot of baboons. And the same drugs don't work in humans."

(sources: src_004, src_009, src_016)

## Using Matrigel for Reproducible Human Tissue Engineering
Utilizing mouse sarcoma-derived Matrigel introduces lot-to-lot variability, ill-defined growth factors (such as TGF-beta), and rapid degradation that corrupts pathway analysis.

(sources: src_009, src_012, src_016)

## Staging Disease Severity Purely by Anatomical Lesion Size
Assessing disease progression or patient pain using physical lesion dimensions or surgical staging fails because intra-pelvic lesion size does not correlate with symptoms or pathway activation.

> "It's not one disease. It's not a disease where the size of your lesions dictate everything. There's molecular subtypes, and we really need to be able to figure them out."

(sources: src_004, src_009)

## Gaslighting Patient Pain as Psychological Stress
Attributing severe physical pelvic symptoms to emotional stress, low pain tolerance, or non-medical causes delays diagnosis by years and allows inflammatory disease to progress unnoticed.

(sources: src_004, src_015, src_044)

## Using PDMS Materials for Microfluidic Drug Screening
Building microfluidic organ-on-a-chip testing platforms using polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS) corrupts pharmacokinetic and efficacy data because PDMS absorbs lipophilic small-molecule compounds.

(sources: src_009)

## Treating Inflammatory Tissue Diseases Solely with Hormone Suppression
Relying exclusively on broad progestin or hormone suppression treatments fails to address underlying progesterone resistance and produces severe side effects without targeting root immune drivers.

(sources: src_000, src_044)

## Optimizing Biological Drugs Solely for Maximum Binding Affinity
Engineering therapeutic molecules solely to maximize cell surface receptor binding without accounting for endosomal dynamics leads to lysosomal degradation rather than endosomal recycling.

(sources: src_009)

## Over-Designing Biological Platforms into Expensive Artwork
Adding unnecessary technical features and microfluidic complexity to tissue platforms creates fragile, costly systems that pharmaceutical companies cannot adopt.

> "Because you can make it really complicated. But we also know that sometimes it's so complicated, it's only art work."

(sources: src_008)
