# Mental Models of Linda Griffith

Conceptual lenses and mental models used by Linda Griffith to diagnose biological systems and design engineering solutions.

## Living Patient Avatars in the Lab
Viewing microphysiological organ-on-a-chip platforms as living human patient surrogates that move clinical trial dynamics into the laboratory phase. Rather than relying on non-human animal physiology, engineered 3D human tissue constructs allow controlled observation of complex organ interactions and drug safety prior to human testing.

(sources: src_004, src_008, src_009)

## Period Privilege
A social bias where individuals who experience mild or symptom-free menstrual cycles passively fail to comprehend or actively dismiss severe gynecological pathology. This societal bias leads healthy individuals and clinicians to normalize debilitating symptoms, delaying medical diagnosis and research funding.

> "I think period privilege is one of those things where women who don't have menstrual problems don't believe the women who do."

(sources: src_004, src_009)

## Cellular Signal Processing Circuit
Conceptualizing cell surface receptors and intracellular pathways as analog circuit components (like transistors and op-amps) that process external cues into physical responses. Extracellular signals like cytokines act as inputs that are integrated through physical circuits inside the cell to generate macroscopic outputs such as cell migration or matrix remodeling.

> "So they process it through a bunch of signals much like the transistors and op-amps and things inside a radio transmits cues from the outside into something that processes information that yields a response."

(sources: src_035)

## Parsing the Mess
Deconstructing chaotic, highly heterogeneous in vivo microenvironments into isolated, defined biophysical and biochemical variables in vitro. Instead of being overwhelmed by patient-to-patient biological variation, engineers isolate individual components (stiffness, hormone exposure, immune cell influx) to dissect disease mechanisms.

> "so we've got a real mess what we'd love to do is parse this mess and bring invitro models up to a state that we could mimic features of these lesions to test what's going on"

(sources: src_003, src_012)

## Move Over Mice
Viewing animal models not as the absolute gold standard, but as limited historical tools that must yield to human microphysiological systems for complex immunology. Recognizing that while animal models captured historical drug dynamics, species differences in immune and tissue biology require human cell platforms for translational accuracy.

(sources: src_016)

## The 3COM / Bob Metcalfe Ethos for Robust Design
Designing technology to survive worst-case operational stress testing and industry constraints right from inception. Engineers must design devices that function under harsh real-world conditions rather than optimizing purely for convenient academic laboratory environments.

> "So this is the MIT ethos to think about what is MIT hard, how do we build around that."

(sources: src_009)
